About our company

Elite Water Filtration is a full-service water treatment company dedicated to providing clean, reliable, great-tasting water to homes across East Texas. With over 20 years of experience in the industry, we specialize in the installation and servicing of water softeners, iron filtration systems, and complete whole-home water treatment solutions tailored to each customer's needs.

Elite Water Filtration es una empresa de tratamiento de agua de servicio completo, dedicada a brindar agua limpia, confiable y de excelente sabor a hogares de todo el Este de Texas. Con más de 20 años de experiencia en la industria, nos especializamos en la instalación y mantenimiento de suavizadores de agua, sistemas de filtración de hierro y soluciones completas de tratamiento de agua para toda la casa, adaptadas a las necesidades de cada cliente.

Servimos con orgullo a Kilgore, Tyler, Canton, Lindale, Athens y las comunidades cercanas del Este de Texas, ofreciendo presupuestos gratuitos y un servicio honesto y confiable en el que puede confiar.

Our services

Whole-House Water Filtration & Sulfur Odor Removal System

Whole-house pH neutralization tank with an air injection system designed to remove hydrogen sulfide (H₂S) and eliminate rotten egg odors from well water.

Whole-House pH Neutralization & Iron Removal System

A whole-house pH neutralization tank combined with an iron removal filtration system to improve water quality throughout the home.

Whole-House Water Softener System

Water softener installation that removes hardness minerals, including calcium and magnesium, to protect plumbing, appliances, and improve water quality.
